## 🧐 What Does This App Do for You?

Think of ats-resume-scorer as your personal career coach that never sleeps. You paste a job description into the app, upload your resume (PDF or Word), and the AI does the rest. Here's exactly what you get back in seconds:

- **ATS Compatibility Score** – A percentage from 0 to 100 showing how likely a robot will read your resume correctly.
 Low scores mean your layout might confuse parsers.

- **Skills Validation** – The app compares every skill mentioned in the job posting against your resumeand flags which ones are missing or underemphasized.

- **Keyword Gap Analysis** – It shows you the exact phrases and buzzwords recruiters look for that you haven't included yet–so you can tailor your resume for each specific job quickly.

- **Plain-Language Recommendations** – No techspeak. You get simple advice like "Add 'project management' near the top of your experience section" or "Your resume layout uses two columns, which confuses many ATS systems–switch to a single column"."

## 💡 Pro Tips for Best Results

- **Run every resume against 2-3 different job descriptions** before you start applying. Patterns will emerge–you'll see the same skills missing over again, and you can fix those on your master template.‎


- **Don't lie–rephrase.** The app doesn't tell you to fabricate experience. It shows you phrases from the job ad that you can legitimately work into your real experience bullets. For example, if "stakeholder communication" is flagged missing and you've actually emailed clients monthly–reword that bullet to say "Provided regular stakeholder communication via status reports". That's honest tailoring, not lying.‎


- **Format matters more than content for the score.** If your score is low but you know you're qualified, the layout is probably the culprit. One-column, standard fonts (Arial, Calibri, Georgia), no tables, no graphics–that's what the ATS robots digest best. The app will tell you exactly what's wrong.‎

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting & FAQ

### ❓ The app won't open after double-clicking

Make sure you extracted the zip first–the app won't run from inside a compressed folder. Also, check that your Windows is 64-bit (Settings → System → About→ look for "64-bit operating system"). If unsure, try right-clicking the .exe → "Run as administrator".



### ❓ My resume parsed with garbled text

Your PDF might use unusual fonts or embedded images. Convert your resume to .docx and try again. The app recommends `.docx` as the most reliable format.

.



### ❓ I get an error saying "API connection failed"

The AI features require a stable internet connection. Check your Wi-Fi–or if you're using VPN, try disconnecting it temporarily. If the issue persists, wait 10 minutes and retry–the free AI servers occasionally rate-limit bursts of usage.

.



### ❓ How is my data handled?

All analysis happens in real-time without storing your resume permanently. Your files are processed in memory, sent over a secure (HTTPS) connection tothe AI engine, which returns scores immediately. We do not train models on your resumes, and you can delete the app anytime–nothing lingers on your machine.



### ❓ Is there a fee?

No–this is open-source software, free for anyone to use forever. The AI processing costs are covered by community donations and sponsorship. If you find it useful, consider starring the repository on GitHub to help visibility.‎

## 📊 What Makes This Different from Other Resume Checkers?

Most free online resume checkers only look for keywords–they're glorified "find and replace" tools. This app uses three layers of intelligence:

- **NLP (Natural Language Processing)** – Actually understands sentence context, not just matching strings. It recognizes that "led a team of 5" means leadership, even if the word "leadership" never appears.‎
- **Sentence Transformers** – Popularly called "AI embeddings", this maps meaning–it catches synonyms and reworded phrasesautomatically. If the job says "collaborated cross-functionally" and your resume says "worked with multiple departments", the AI recognizes those as the same skill.‎
- **LLM (Large Language Model) Analysis** – The System produces human-readable, tailored advice, not generic tips. Each recommendation is specific to your resume's actual wording–like having a professional resume writer look over your shoulder.‎
